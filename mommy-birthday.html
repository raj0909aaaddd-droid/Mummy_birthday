If I Could Give You One Thing…

Mommy,

I've started this letter so many times, and each time I stop, because nothing I write feels big enough. So I'll just say it plainly, the way I wish I said it more often.

Thank you for raising me. Thank you for every night you stayed up, every meal you made while exhausted, and every time you put yourself last so I could come first. You protected me from things I never even knew were there, and I think I'm only now seeing how much you carried on your own.

Thank you for believing in me, even when I gave you very little reason to. There were days I doubted myself completely, and your faith never moved. I don't think you know how many times that was the only thing holding me up.

I'm sorry, too. I'm sorry for the times I snapped, for the times I was careless with your feelings, for the times I disappointed you and was too proud to say sorry. I never meant to hurt you. I hope you know that.

I may not always say it out loud, but I notice everything. I notice when you're tired. I notice what you do for all of us without being asked. I notice, and I'm grateful, even on the days I forget to show it.

I hope I can make you proud. That's the quiet wish behind most of what I do. And someday I want to give you a beautiful life, a calm, comfortable, happy one, where you finally get to rest and be the one who is looked after.

No matter how old I get, I will always be your child. Taller, maybe, and busier, but still yours.

I love you, Mommy.
More than I know how to explain.

Your son, Raj ♡
